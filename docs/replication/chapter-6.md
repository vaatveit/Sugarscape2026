## Choices

- **Credit on two goods:** loans are in sugar. Fertility tests sugar only (p.55, user decision 2026-10-21), so nobody falls short of spice to have a child, and a spice loan book would stay empty.
- **VI-1:** Chapter IV agents (200 starters, as frame 6 shows), tags 11, K, T, L(10, 10%), and E with V-2's endemic setting (25 diseases, 10 per agent; our call). Cultural preferences and inheritance are off, because the rule set names neither.
- **VI-2 / VI-3:** 500 Chapter IV agents: vision 1–5, metabolisms 1–5, endowments 25–50, max age 60–100 (the "eighty years on average"), childbearing as in Fig IV-14. The runs differ only in T, so seed k of each starts from the identical society, and the "decline as without trade" row compares them seed by seed.
- **Schelling:** von Neumann neighbors on a 50×50 torus. An agent with no neighbors counts as satisfied. A dissatisfied agent moves to a random acceptable empty site anywhere on the lattice (p.166), or stays if there is none, and newcomers go to a random site that satisfies them. Segregation is measured as `like_fraction`, the mean share of like neighbors (≈0.5 at random).
- **Ring World:** 150 sites, integer sugar 0–4, capacity 4, unit growback after all moves. 40 agents with vision 15–30 look counterclockwise only. Unlike the main model's M, an agent's own site is not a candidate: "each agent moves once each time period" (p.172), so an agent with any free site in view moves, even onto a site with no more sugar than its own. It stays only when its whole view is occupied (p.175). A group is a run of agents with at most one empty site between neighbors.
- **Comparisons:** the book states several Chapter VI results as comparisons, so those targets are paired-seed ratios (`Target.baseline`).

   - **With trade**, the book's shape (a near-extinction followed by recovery) appears, but later and smaller. The society declines a little *further* than without trade (≈128 at t=100), bottoms out at ≈70–100 around t≈100–200, and climbs to ≈650–700 by t≈300–450. Then it stays flat. There are no 700–1200 waves: the ceiling is ≈720, the floor after t=250 is ≈300 because recovery is still under way in slow seeds, and a period is found in only 2 of 5 seeds.
