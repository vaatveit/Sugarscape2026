# Sugarscape xxl, stage 6: sex, Part 1 — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Readings that say whether mate finding limits births in xxl more than the book's rules do at the book's
scale, and whether selection acts. The readings come from VI-1 in three worlds (book, xl, today), plus a report that
applies bars M and S.

**Architecture:**
- **Mating log.** An opt-in log in rule S (`Model.mate_log`, `nothing` by default) records each mating attempt that
  finds a fertile partner of the other sex.
- **Observer.** `SexReadings`, like stage 5's `TradeReadings`, reads that log and the agents each period. It writes a
  per-period table and a snapshot of agents whose fertile life ended in the window.
- **Study and report.** A study command runs a world's 20 seeds with the observer, and a report command reads the
  three worlds' folders.

**Tech Stack:** Julia (`julia/`, module `Sugarscape`), `Test`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-xxl-sex-design.md` (approved by the user 2026-11-07). Read it first.

## Global Constraints

- **Branch:** `xxl-sex` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/xxl-sex`), from main `c0b0312`. Merging and pushing are the
  user's calls.

  digests and `julia/test/fixtures/xxl-v1/*.csv` pass unchanged. With them on, each seed's standard CSV is
  byte-identical to the run without them. The observer's random draws come from its own copy of the RNG, as the
  scorecard's X2 count does (`push_fertility!`, `julia/src/xxl/scorecard_metrics.jl`).
- **Rules stay as they are:**
  - no change to the fertility test, endowments, childbearing ages or gifts;
  - `XXL_DEFAULTS` and `XXL_V1` don't change;
  - Python, the runner's output columns and the viewer don't change.

- **Before each commit to `julia/src`,** run the suite in the background:
  `julia --project=julia -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.test()'` (12–25 min).
- **Style:** files LF; docs in `docs/xxl.md`'s plain style.

## Review Focus

1. **An agent near the torus edge with vision 50 (xl or today):** the count of mates within vision wraps around, and
   finds a mate on the far side. Test in Task 2, on a small hand-made world.
2. **A book seed that dies out, or has no fertile agent-periods in the window (200 founders):** it is left out of that
   reading and counted. With fewer than 15 seeds left, the bar reads missing, and the outcome follows the spec's
   missing rules. Test in Task 4.
3. **Worlds without speed (book, xl):** speed columns are NaN, the report shows "—", and nothing errors. Test in
   Tasks 2 and 4.
4. **Agents who die before reaching childbearing age:** they never enter the snapshot. An agent whose fertile age ends
   inside the window enters it once, with its end reason. Test in Task 2.
5. **A stopped run started again:** a seed cut short is removed and rerun from period 0, and a folder made under
   another world, code or periods is refused before anything runs. Test in Task 3.

### Task 2: The sex readings

**Files:**
- Create: `julia/src/xxl/sex_readings.jl`, included after `trade_readings.jl`.
- Modify: `julia/src/runner.jl` (a `sex_readings=nothing` keyword on `timed_seed` and `seed_job`, called like
  `trade_period!`, refused with a checkpoint).
- Create: `julia/test/test_xxl_sex_readings.jl`.

**Interfaces:**
- **Consumes** Task 1's `MateEvent` and `Model.mate_log`.
- **Produces** `SexReadings(; window::Tuple{Int,Int})` and `sex_period!(r::SexReadings, m::Model)`, called every period
  from t = 0:
  - At its first call it copies `m.rng` for its own draws and sets `m.mate_log = MateEvent[]`.
  - Each call reads that period's events, then empties the log.
- **Produces** `sex_rows(r) -> (header, rows)`, one row per period from t = 0 to the end, with `SEX_COLUMNS`:
  - `t`, `population`;
  - `eligible`: of childbearing age, sex not `:none`;
  - `fertile`: passes `is_fertile` at the config's threshold;
  - `fertile_reach`: has a fertile mate of the other sex in `mate_reach`, looked at with the observer's RNG, as
    `push_fertility!` does;
  - `fertile_vision`: has one anywhere within vision. The shape is the four lattice lines, every site 1 to V, when
    the look is the lattice look (book, xl). It is the disc 0 < dx² + dy² ≤ V², torus distance, under round vision
    (today). The count is exact. The method is the builder's (a bucket grid is fine);
  - `births`, `no_site`: this period's events by `born`;
  - `mean_distance`, `median_distance`, `mean_distance_share`, `median_distance_share`: over the period's `born`
    events, where share = distance ÷ seeker's vision;
  - `mean_vision`, `sd_vision`, `mean_metabolism`, `sd_metabolism`, `mean_spice_metabolism`,
    `sd_spice_metabolism`, `mean_speed`, `sd_speed`.

  A column with nothing to average is NaN. Speed is NaN in a world without speed.
- **Produces** `sex_agent_rows(r) -> (header, rows)`, the snapshot. It holds every agent whose fertile life ends inside
  `window`. An agent's fertile life ends when its age reaches its fertility end (end reason 0), or when it dies after
  reaching childbearing age (end reason 1). `SEX_AGENT_COLUMNS`:
  - `id`, `sex` (0 F, 1 M), `end_t`, `end_reason`;
  - `periods_eligible`, `periods_fertile`, `periods_fertile_reach`, `periods_fertile_vision` (counted over the agent's
    whole life);
  - `children`, `vision`, `metabolism`, `speed` (NaN without speed).
- **Produces** `const SEX_WINDOW = 200`: the snapshot covers the last 200 periods, t = 301–500 for 500 periods.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** on hand-made worlds:
  - A fertile pair at distance 20 under round vision with vision 30, outside the look's sample (choose the seed so the
    look misses): `fertile_reach == 0`, `fertile_vision == 2`.
  - A pair across the torus edge, at distance 5 the short way: counted within vision.
  - On the lattice, a pair on a line at distance 4 with vision 5 is counted; one off every line is not.
  - `births` and `no_site` match the log.
  - `mean_distance` matches a hand birth at distance 3.
  - Speed columns are NaN in a book-config world.
  - An agent dying at age 5 (before childbearing) never appears in the snapshot. One aging out at t = 310 appears
    once with `end_reason == 0`, and its period counts equal hand counts.
  - On a 12-period VI-1 run in each of book, xl and `XXL_DEFAULTS`, the standard CSV is byte-identical with and
    without the observer.
  - On a short run, `fertile` equals the scorecard's `n_fertile`.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run them to see them fail.** Expected: `SexReadings` not defined.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.**

### Task 4: The sex report

**Files:**
- Create: `julia/src/xxl/sex_report.jl`.
- Create: `julia/xxl_sex_report.jl`.
- Create: `julia/test/test_xxl_sex_report.jl`.

**Interfaces:**
- **Consumes** Task 3's folders and `bootstrap_ratio_apart(run, base; reps=BOOT_REPS, seed=BOOT_SEED)`
  (`julia/src/xxl/disease_list.jl`).
- **Produces** `sex_report(; book, xl, today, md::AbstractString, io=stdout)`. It refuses a folder whose fingerprint
  doesn't name the world it is passed as.
- **Produces** the command line `julia --project=julia julia/xxl_sex_report.jl --book DIR --xl DIR --today DIR [--md PATH]`,
  with `--md` defaulting to `results-julia-xxl/sex-part1.md`.
- **The bar constants,** the spec's values: `M_FACTOR = 1.5`, `MIN_FERTILE_PERIODS = 5`, `TROUGH_HALF = 25`,
  `MIN_SEEDS = 15`, `S_SHARE = 0.5`, `S_MIN_BOOK_DROP = 0.1`.
- **Per-seed values,** each a seed's totals in the window, not a mean of per-period shares. The late window is
  t = 301–500, or the window in the fingerprint:
  - **No-mate share:** 1 − Σ `fertile_reach` ÷ Σ `fertile`.
  - **Childless share:** among snapshot agents with `periods_fertile >= 5`, the share with `children == 0`.
  - **Metabolism drop:** (mean metabolism at t = 0 − at the last period) ÷ at t = 0, from the standard CSV.
  - **Trough no-mate share:** the trough is the seed's lowest population over t ≥ 1 (first if tied). The window is
    trough ± 25, clipped to the run.
  - **Readings,** shown and deciding nothing:
    - eligible share; fertile share; wealth-blocked share, 1 − Σ `fertile` ÷ Σ `eligible`;
    - no-mate-within-vision share;
    - births per fertile agent-period; `no_site` per birth;
    - mean and median parent distance, and as a share of vision;
    - start and end of mean and sd vision, metabolism, spice metabolism and speed.

  A seed that reached population 0, or has a zero denominator, is left out of that value and counted in the report.
- **The bars:**
  - **M1, M2:** today ÷ book with `bootstrap_ratio_apart`. Each holds when the ratio is ≥ `M_FACTOR` and the
    interval's lower end is > 1. Fewer than `MIN_SEEDS` seeds on either side reads missing. M holds when both hold.
    xl ÷ book and today ÷ xl are shown beside them. The trough M1 is shown, deciding nothing.
  - **S:** the ratio of mean drops, today ÷ book, with its interval shown.
    - It decides on the point value ≥ `S_SHARE`.
    - If the book's mean drop is under `S_MIN_BOOK_DROP`, S reads "not judged" and decides nothing.
    - It reads missing on fewer than `MIN_SEEDS` seeds.
- **The outcome line,** by the spec's rules, in this order:
  1. S fails or reads missing: report to the user, no rule.
  2. M reads missing: no outcome.
  3. M holds: a rule spec goes to the user.
  4. Otherwise: sex works as intended.
- **The md** lists each world's folder, seeds used and left out, the bars (value, interval, bar, result), the readings
  table and the outcome.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** on hand-written folders, with fingerprints, standard CSVs, `-sex.csv` and
  `-agents.csv` files:
  - M1 and M2 read the hand values, e.g. today's no-mate share 0.6 against the book's 0.3 → ratio 2.0, holds with
    tight seeds.
  - A case where the ratio is ≥ 1.5 but the interval's low end is ≤ 1 doesn't hold.
  - S with the book's drop 0.05 reads "not judged".
  - S with today's drop 0.2 against the book's 0.6 fails, and the outcome is "report to the user".
  - Six book seeds at population 0 leave 14 seeds: M reads missing, and the left-out seeds are counted.
  - Speed reads "—" for book and xl.
  - A folder passed as `--xl` whose fingerprint names `book` is refused.
  - The trough window is clipped at t = 1.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run them to see them fail.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.**
- [ ] **Step 4: Run the report tests, then the suite.** Expected: pass.
